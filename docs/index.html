What happens to the sound

1 · Capture10 s of mono audio at 16 kHz, in a rolling buffer.
2 · ChromaSTFT, then a 12-band chroma filterbank → a 12 × 313 map.
3 · EncodeA convolutional autoencoder compresses 312 frames to 78.
4 · ClassifyA four-block CNN outputs one siren probability.

The encoder and classifier are the .keras files from the repository, exported to JSON and run with a small forward pass written for this page — same weights, same architecture, no inference library.

Honest limits

This is the cnn_chroma model, chosen because it is the smallest; it was the weakest of the nine trained in the thesis, and chroma is a deliberately lossy pitch-class representation. Expect false positives on anything tonal and sweeping — alarms, some music, a kettle. The paper reports full results across MFCC, LFCC and chroma with FFNN, CNN and LSTM heads.

One deviation from the Python pipeline: librosa.feature.chroma_stft estimates a tuning offset per signal and shifts its filterbank accordingly. This page ships the untuned filterbank, which moves the output probability by less than 0.01 on test signals. The network itself matches Keras to eight decimals.
